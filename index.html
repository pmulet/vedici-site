The firm

Vedici researches, designs and runs systematic strategies across financial markets. Every decision starts as a hypothesis and is accepted only after it survives data, simulation and risk control.

We are a long-term undertaking. The firm is built to be owned by the people who build it, and to compound knowledge as patiently as it compounds capital.

Approach

We develop models

Statistical and machine-learning models of price, volatility and risk, tested out of sample before they touch capital.

We automate investment

Execution is systematic and disciplined. Position sizing follows the mathematics, not the mood of the day.

We provide intelligence

Processed financial data, risk simulations and actionable signals for institutions that need decisions tailored to their portfolios.

We invest in people

Scientists, engineers and operators who share our values. Talent is the only edge that compounds without limit.

Veni, vidi, vici.

The name

In 47 BC, after the battle of Zela, Julius Caesar reported his victory to the Roman Senate in three words. Vedici takes its name from them: a reminder that speed and clarity, applied with discipline, win.
